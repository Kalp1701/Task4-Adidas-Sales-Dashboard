# Task 4 – Dashboard Design (Adidas Sales Performance)

## Objective

The objective of this task was to design an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders using Power BI. The focus was on selecting relevant KPIs, creating meaningful visualizations, and presenting data in a clear and structured way to support decision-making.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Adidas Sales Dataset (Kaggle)

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a summary of Adidas sales performance with key business metrics and visual insights. It includes interactive filters and multiple visualizations to analyze sales from different perspectives.

### Key KPIs:

* Total Sales
* Average Operating Margin
* Total Units Sold
* Total Operating Profit

## Visualizations Included

* Regional Sales Performance (sales comparison across regions)
* Sales vs Profit Analysis by Product Category
* Sales Distribution by Product Category
* Sales Trend Over Time (time-series analysis)
* Sales Channel Distribution (online, outlet, in-store)

## Interactivity

* Slicers added for Quarter and City to allow filtering and exploration.
* Users can analyze trends based on different selections.

## Key Insights

* Some regions contribute significantly higher sales compared to others.
* Certain product categories generate higher revenue.
* Sales trend shows variation across time periods.
* Sales channels contribute differently to total performance.
